How to make a quiz from a video

You can turn a video lesson into an active study session in a few steps. Instead of replaying the whole video and hoping you remember it, use a quiz to check what you understood.

Make your first video quiz →

Step 1: Choose a useful lesson

Find a YouTube video that covers the topic you want to study. Make sure the video is accessible and that its spoken or displayed content is relevant to your learning goal.

Step 2: Generate the questions

Open Video Quiz, paste the YouTube URL, choose 5, 8, or 12 questions, and select Make quiz. Wait while the AI analyzes the source and prepares the questions.

Step 3: Answer, learn, and retry

Choose an answer for each question. If it is incorrect, read the explanation and try again. Missed questions come back in a review round. Use timestamp links when available to revisit the lesson.

Step 4: Review the source

After finishing, look at the concepts you found difficult and replay those parts of the lesson. For important coursework, compare generated questions with your class notes and the original source.

Frequently asked questions

What makes video quizzes useful for studying?

They encourage active recall: you try to retrieve information instead of only watching or rereading it.

Can I make a quiz from a lecture recording?

If the recording is available as an accessible YouTube video, you can paste its link into the app.

Can I use a PDF instead of a video?

Yes. Choose Files or photos on the homepage and upload supported study materials.
