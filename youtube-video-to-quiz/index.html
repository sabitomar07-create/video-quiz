Turn a YouTube video into a quiz

Watching a lesson is a start. Testing yourself helps you find out what you actually understood. Video Quiz turns a YouTube video into multiple-choice questions you can answer and review.

Create a quiz from a YouTube video →

How it works

Copy the URL of a YouTube video, open Video Quiz, paste the link, and choose 5, 8, or 12 questions. Select Make quiz and let the video analysis finish. Then answer each question and read the explanations.

Learn from the explanations

When you choose an incorrect answer, Video Quiz explains the option and lets you try again. Questions you miss return in a review round, so you can revisit the ideas that need more attention.

Go back to the source

When a question has a video timestamp, you can open the lesson near the point where the concept appears. Use that moment to replay the explanation and strengthen your understanding.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to write the questions myself?

No. Paste a supported YouTube link and the AI generates the quiz questions from the video.

Can I choose the number of questions?

Yes. The current app offers 5, 8, or 12 questions.

Can I review questions I got wrong?

Yes. Incorrect choices show explanations, and missed questions return in a review round.
