Background processing for longer lessons

After you submit a YouTube link, Video Quiz starts video analysis and checks for the result while processing continues. Longer analysis can take more time, so keep the quiz page open while it works.

Questions grounded in the lesson

The quiz prompt asks the AI to study the lesson as a whole and distribute questions across its available content. The result still depends on the video's accessibility, the AI service, and the source material.

A practical way to review

Choose 5, 8, or 12 questions, answer them, and read the explanations. If a question includes a timestamp, use its rewatch link to return to the relevant part of the video.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an unlimited video length?

No unlimited duration is promised. Processing time and supported duration depend on the video and the AI service.

How long does analysis take?

It varies with the source video and service availability. Longer videos can take longer to process.

Can I use it with short videos too?

Yes. The same YouTube quiz workflow can be used for shorter lessons and tutorials.
